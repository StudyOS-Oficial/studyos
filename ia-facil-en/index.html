A few useful details

Questions before you start?

Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. It starts with the essentials and builds step by step. You do not need a technical background.

How long does it take?

Allow approximately 5–7 hours for the course. The actual time depends on how much you practise and how often you pause to explore examples.

Do I have to follow the modules in order?

The sequence is designed to build skills gradually, but you can also open a module directly when you need that topic.

Will AI make decisions for me?

No. The course focuses on using AI as support while you stay responsible for checking important information and making your own decisions.
