MODULE 09 · 10 LESSONS

Organize your life with AI

Turn tasks, priorities, routines and plans into practical systems while keeping control of your time and decisions.

LESSON 1

Start with everything on your mind

When a list feels overwhelming, paste the tasks into AI and ask it to organise them without inventing new obligations.

Try it: Prompt: 'Group this list into urgent, important and optional. Do not delete anything and do not add tasks.'
LESSON 2

Prioritise realistically

Not everything can be urgent. Give AI deadlines, consequences, effort and dependencies, then ask it to propose an order.

Try it: Review the proposed order yourself. AI does not know the emotional or practical consequences unless you explain them.
LESSON 3

Build a simple routine

AI can convert recurring responsibilities into a weekly routine. Tell it what is fixed and what is flexible.

Try it: Ask for a plan with buffer time. A schedule that leaves no room for reality is not a useful schedule.
LESSON 4

Plan a project

For a larger goal, ask AI to break it into milestones, tasks and dependencies. Then identify the first action you can actually do.

Try it: Prompt: 'Turn this goal into four milestones. For each, give me the next physical action and a reasonable time estimate.'
LESSON 5

Prepare for a busy week

Give AI your fixed appointments, deadlines and priorities. Ask for a draft schedule that protects rest and travel time.

Try it: Do not include sensitive calendar information that is unnecessary. General descriptions are usually enough.
LESSON 6

Create checklists

Checklists are excellent for repeatable tasks: travel preparation, weekly shopping, home maintenance or preparing a meeting.

Try it: Ask for a checklist that is short enough to use. Then edit it until it matches your real routine.
LESSON 7

Reduce decision fatigue

For repeated low-risk decisions, create rules. AI can help you design them, such as a meal rotation or packing checklist.

Try it: A good system reduces the number of decisions you need to make, but you should still review the rules when circumstances change.
LESSON 8

Prepare conversations and meetings

AI can help you build an agenda, questions, talking points or a concise summary. Keep confidential information out unless it is genuinely necessary and appropriate.

Try it: Prompt: 'Turn these notes into a 20-minute meeting agenda with three decisions we need to make.'
LESSON 9

Review and adapt

A plan is a tool, not a promise. Ask AI to review what worked, what failed and what should change next week.

Try it: A useful weekly review has three questions: What worked? What got in the way? What is one change worth testing?
LESSON 10

Final challenge

Give AI a messy list of ten real but non-sensitive tasks. Ask it to prioritise them, build a realistic sequence and identify the first three actions.

Try it: Then remove one task and see how the plan changes. You are learning to use AI as a planning assistant—not as the person in charge of your life.
Do it now: Apply this module to one real, low-risk situation in your life. Finish the task rather than just reading about it.
