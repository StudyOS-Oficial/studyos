MODULE 04 · 10 LESSONS

AI in everyday life

Turn AI into a practical assistant for ordinary situations: planning, cooking, travel, messages, shopping, learning and decisions.

LESSON 1

Plan your day

AI can turn a list of obligations into a realistic plan. Tell it your available hours, fixed appointments, priorities and energy constraints.

Practical idea: Example: 'I have three hours this afternoon, a doctor's appointment at 17:00 and four tasks. Help me prioritise them, include breaks and leave 20 minutes of buffer time.' Treat the result as a draft, not a command.
LESSON 2

Cooking and meal ideas

Give AI the ingredients you already have, dietary preferences, number of people and time available. It can suggest recipes, substitutions and a shopping list.

Practical idea: Prompt: 'I have eggs, tomatoes, rice and spinach. Create two simple dinners for two people, under 30 minutes. Tell me what one extra ingredient would improve each.' Always verify food-safety details when relevant.
LESSON 3

Travel planning

AI is useful for brainstorming itineraries and identifying questions to investigate. Tell it dates, departure point, interests, mobility needs, budget and pace.

Practical idea: Ask for an itinerary first, then verify opening hours, transport schedules, prices and entry requirements with official sources before booking anything.
LESSON 4

Writing everyday messages

You can ask AI to make a message clearer without losing your voice. Give the relationship and desired tone.

Practical idea: Example: 'Rewrite this message to my neighbour. Keep it polite and calm, explain the issue clearly and avoid sounding aggressive.' Read the final text before sending it—your name is on the message, not the AI's.
LESSON 5

Shopping and comparisons

AI can help you define what matters before buying. Ask it to create a comparison checklist or explain technical specifications in plain language.

Practical idea: For expensive purchases, use AI to prepare questions and compare information, then check current prices, warranties and specifications on reliable retailer or manufacturer sources.
LESSON 6

Learning something new

AI can become a tutor if you tell it your level and preferred learning method. Ask for explanations, examples, questions and feedback rather than just answers.

Practical idea: Try: 'Teach me the basics of photography in five short lessons. After each lesson, give me one practical exercise and wait for my answer before continuing.'
LESSON 7

Making decisions

AI can structure a decision without making it for you. Ask for criteria, trade-offs, risks and questions you may have overlooked.

Practical idea: Prompt: 'Help me compare these two options. Do not choose for me. Build a decision table and tell me what information would change the recommendation.'
LESSON 8

Organising information

Paste a messy list and ask AI to group, prioritise or convert it into a checklist. Remove personal data that is not necessary.

Practical idea: For example: 'Organise these errands by location and urgency. Do not add tasks that are not in my list.' This is a simple way to save time without handing over control.
LESSON 9

Creative everyday uses

AI can help with invitations, gift ideas, stories, hobbies, garden plans, activities with children and many other low-risk creative tasks.

Practical idea: The more personal the result should feel, the more you should add your own preferences. AI supplies possibilities; you supply taste and judgement.
LESSON 10

Final practical challenge

Choose one real task from your week. Use AI to plan or draft it, then improve the result with one follow-up and verify anything important.

Practical idea: Write down the time you think the task would have taken without AI and the time it actually took. The goal of this course is not to use AI everywhere; it is to know where it genuinely helps.
Do it now: Apply the technique to one real situation from your own life. Keep the task simple enough to finish in 10–15 minutes.
FINAL CHECK

Put the module into practice

Before moving on, answer these questions for yourself:

  1. What is the most useful idea from this module?
  2. Where could you apply it this week?
  3. What would you need to verify before trusting the result?

Remember: AI is most useful when you stay in control, provide good instructions and review what comes back.
