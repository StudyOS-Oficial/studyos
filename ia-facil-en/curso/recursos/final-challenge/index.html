Course challenge

Put it all together

Choose one real situation from your life and take it from beginning to end using what you learned in the course.

Your mission

Pick a task that is useful but not high-risk: planning a trip, organising a week, preparing a meal plan, drafting a message, learning a topic, comparing options or creating a checklist.

1 · Define

What exactly do you want to achieve?

2 · Give context

What information does AI actually need?

3 · Prompt

Ask for a useful format and clear result.

4 · Review

What could be wrong, missing or unclear?

5 · Refine

Ask for a second version based on your feedback.

6 · Decide

You make the final decision and use the result.

Final checklist

You are not trying to become an AI expert.
You are learning to use AI confidently, safely and intentionally when it can genuinely help.
