After the course

What now?

You do not need to learn everything about AI. Build a small habit: use it for real problems, review what it gives you and gradually discover what works for you.

WEEK 1

One useful task a day

Use AI for one real task: a message, a plan, a question or an idea.

WEEK 2

Improve your prompts

Notice which details make the answers better and start adding them intentionally.

WEEK 3

Build one workflow

Choose a recurring task and create a repeatable AI-assisted process.

WEEK 4

Teach someone else

Explain one useful AI habit to a friend or family member. Teaching reinforces learning.

Your personal AI rules

Write down three rules you want to follow when using AI.

1. I will use AI to help me with: __________________________ 2. I will always verify: _________________________________ 3. I will never share: ___________________________________
The goal is confidence, not dependence.
The best result of this course is being able to recognise when AI is useful, ask for what you need and know when to stop and check.
