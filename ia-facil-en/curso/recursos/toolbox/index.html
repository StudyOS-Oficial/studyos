Quick reference · AI Toolbox

Your AI toolbox

A compact reference for the ideas that matter most. Use this page when you do not remember which technique to use.

PROMPTING

Goal + Context + Task + Format

The simplest structure for turning a vague request into a useful instruction.

PROMPTING

Ask for a second pass

Ask AI to critique, simplify, shorten, expand or improve its first answer.

RELIABILITY

Verify important claims

Confidence is not evidence. Check facts when accuracy matters.

PRIVACY

Never share secrets

Do not paste passwords, security codes or unnecessary sensitive information.

LEARNING

Use active recall

Ask AI questions, answer yourself and then request feedback.

PLANNING

Break big tasks down

Turn a large goal into smaller steps, priorities and checkpoints.

THINKING

Challenge assumptions

Ask what might be missing, wrong or based on an assumption.

COMMUNICATION

Set the audience and tone

Tell AI who the message is for and how you want it to sound.

Three rules to remember

1. AI helps; you decide.
2. Clear instructions produce better results.
3. Important information deserves verification.
