Extra resource · Prompt Kit

Ready-to-use prompts

A practical collection of prompts you can copy, adapt and reuse in everyday situations. The goal is not to memorise prompts — it is to learn how to turn a simple request into a useful instruction.

Before you copy

Replace the words in [brackets] with your own details. Then ask a follow-up question if the first answer is not quite right.
TRAVEL

Plan a trip

Build a realistic itinerary without overloading the day.

I am travelling to [place] for [number] days. My budget is [budget]. I enjoy [interests] and I prefer [walking/short journeys/etc.]. Create a realistic daily plan with free time, approximate costs and practical tips.
MESSAGES

Write a message

Change the tone without losing your meaning.

Rewrite this message so it sounds [warm/professional/direct/friendly]. Keep the meaning, remove unnecessary words and make it sound natural: [paste message]
COOKING

Use what I have

Turn ingredients into a practical meal idea.

I have these ingredients: [list]. Suggest 3 simple meals. Give ingredients, steps, approximate time and what I can substitute if I am missing something.
SHOPPING

Compare options

Structure a purchase decision before spending money.

I am choosing between [options]. My priorities are [price/quality/durability/etc.]. Compare them in a table, identify the main trade-offs and tell me what type of buyer each option suits.
ORGANISATION

Make a plan

Turn a messy list into clear next steps.

Here is everything I need to do: [list]. Group the tasks, identify the most important ones and create a realistic plan for [today/this week]. Do not schedule more than I can reasonably complete.
LEARNING

Learn a topic

Ask AI to teach rather than simply give the answer.

Teach me [topic] as a beginner. Explain it simply, give me one everyday example, ask me 3 questions to check my understanding and then correct my answers.

The universal prompt

My goal is: [goal].

Context: [important information].

Please: [exact task].

Format: [list/table/steps/etc.].

Keep it: [simple/brief/detailed].
