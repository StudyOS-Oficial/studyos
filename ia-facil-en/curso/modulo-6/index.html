Do not believe everything at first

Recognize uncertainty, detect errors and verify important information.

LESSON 1

Understand the basic idea

Start with the concept and learn why it matters in everyday life.

LESSON 2

See a real example

Look at a concrete situation before trying it yourself.

LESSON 3

Practice with ChatGPT

Use a short practical prompt and observe the result.

LESSON 4

Improve the first answer

Ask for a clearer, shorter or more useful version instead of starting again.

LESSON 5

Use context

Add the details that actually change the answer.

LESSON 6

Choose a useful format

Ask for bullets, steps, a table or another format that makes the result easy to use.

LESSON 7

Check the result

Check whether the answer really matches your goal and verify important facts.

LESSON 8

Apply it to your own situation

Take the technique and apply it to a task from your own life.

LESSON 9

Build a simple habit

Turn what you learned into a repeatable habit.

LESSON 10

Final challenge

Complete one real task from start to finish using the method from this module.
