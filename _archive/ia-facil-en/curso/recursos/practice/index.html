Practice with AI

Five realistic challenges. Try them yourself first, then open the suggested approach. There is no single perfect answer — the point is to practise giving useful instructions and reviewing the result.

Challenge 1 · The messy message

You need to tell a friend that you cannot attend dinner tonight, but you want to sound warm and suggest another day.

Suggested approach
Write a short, warm WhatsApp message to a friend explaining that I cannot make dinner tonight. Suggest meeting another day. Keep it natural and not overly formal.

Challenge 2 · The overloaded day

You have eight tasks and only a few hours. Ask AI to prioritise them without pretending it knows what matters most to you.

Suggested approach
Here are my tasks: [list]. I have [time] available. My priorities are [priorities]. Help me rank them, explain the trade-offs and create a realistic plan. Ask me one question if essential information is missing.

Challenge 3 · The confusing explanation

Ask AI to explain a difficult topic as if you were a complete beginner, then ask it to test you.

Challenge 4 · The purchase decision

Give AI two products and ask for a comparison based on criteria that matter to you.

Challenge 5 · The second pass

Take any AI answer you have received and ask: “What could be wrong, unclear or missing here?”

Your practice rule

Prompt → Review → Refine.
Good AI use is rarely one perfect prompt. It is a conversation where you improve the result and keep your own judgement.

Five challenges today are better than fifty prompts you never use.
